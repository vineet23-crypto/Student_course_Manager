# Project Statement

## Problem Statement

Managing student and course records manually can be time-consuming and may lead to errors or difficulty in finding and updating information. The project aims to provide a simple Java-based system for managing student, course, and enrollment records in an organized way.

## Scope of the Project

The project focuses on managing basic academic records such as student information, course information, and student-course enrollments. It provides operations to add, view, update, search, and manage records. The system is intended for small-scale academic record management and does not aim to replace a complete university management system.

## Target Users

The system is mainly intended for:

- Students who need to view or manage their course-related information.
- Faculty members who need to maintain basic course and student records.
- Academic or administrative staff who manage student and course information.

## High-Level Features

- Add and manage student records.
- Add and manage course records.
- Enroll students in courses.
- View student and course information.
- Search for records.
- Update existing records.
- Delete records when required.
- Store and manage data using Java-based file/data handling.
- Maintain backup and exported records.
